## Templates

Arrangements of organisms without content of their own.

### WorkspaceLayout

The frame of the workspace: one full-window region for the canvas and everything floating over it, and a place for what must stay usable while that region is blocked (the live region for announcements, modal dialogs, the presenter). It holds no content of its own; the page fills its two slots.

- File: `src/renderer/design-system/templates/WorkspaceLayout.vue`
- Story: `src/renderer/design-system/templates/WorkspaceLayout.stories.ts`

| Prop | Type | Required | Default | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `label` | `string` | yes |  | The accessible name of the workspace landmark. |
| `feedbackHeight` | `number \| undefined` | no | `0` | Height in pixels that feedback at the bottom takes; side panels end above it. |
| `inert` | `boolean \| undefined` | no | `false` | Blocks the content region for pointer and keyboard, while a transition runs or something covers it. |

| Slot | Description |
| --- | --- |
| `default` | Canvas, bars, panels and feedback: everything that belongs to the working surface. |
| `overlays` | What stays reachable while the content is blocked: announcements, dialogs, full-screen modes. |
